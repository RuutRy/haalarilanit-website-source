Entrance to the event is open for <Mark>24h</Mark> a day and it is located near the LAB-University's parking lot. Entrance and doors to toilets are marked on the event map above. Entrance is barrier-free.

During the event the LAB parking lot is free and there will be no parking control. The entire <Mark>P4</Mark> parking area at LAB is free to use throughout the LAN event!

## LAN seat information

- Your LAN seat is <Mark>90 cm</Mark> wide.
- A chair will be provided for you (you may bring your own if you prefer).
- One power outlet is available for your setup (please bring your own extension cord).
- The network port may be several meters away (a <Mark>10 m</Mark> Ethernet cable will definitely be sufficient).

## Essential equipment

Bring all your needed gaming gear but be mindful of the restrictions of your seat

- Computer/Console
- Monitor
- Keyboard/Controller
- Mouse & Mousepad
- Headphones
- Extension cord
- Network cable (10m)
- Phone / Headphone charger
- Cash / Mobilepay
