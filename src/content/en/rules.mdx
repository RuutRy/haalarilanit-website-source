# Rules

## Prohibited items

Do not bring the following into the event area:

- Weapons and other items that can hurt others, such as knives
- Alcohol and other intoxicants (excluding tobacco and nicotine products)
- Explosives and other dangerous substances

## Intoxicants

Using or possessing alcohol or narcotics in the event area is prohibited. Please leave all alcoholic beverages outside the event area.

Smoking and vaping are allowed only outside at the marked smoking zones. Use the cigarette bins provided there.

## Electricity grid

Each gamer gets one electrical outlet. The outlet is limited to an average of <Mark>350 W</Mark> while gaming (computer, monitor, phone charger etc. combined). The limit is about the power your devices actually consume, not the rated wattage printed on your power supply.

Do not connect household appliances or other high-power electrical devices to your outlet.

The event provides a shared microwave and electric kettle. If your setup draws more than <Mark>350 W</Mark> on average, contact the organizers before the event.

## Event area and damage

Damaging or messing up the event area is prohibited. For example, do not put stickers on walls.

If you damage something, you are responsible for covering the cost of the damage.

## Network and internet

You must not do any of the following on the event network:

- Run DHCP servers
- Interfere with network traffic
- Monitor traffic other than your own
- Connect additional devices, such as switches or firewalls
- Retrieve or distribute copyrighted material or material that violates Finnish law

## Sleeping

For safety reasons, sleeping is allowed only at your own seat. Sleeping on the floor or elsewhere in the event area is prohibited.

## General rules

- Follow Finnish law, the event rules, and <SaferSpaceLink text="LTKY's safer space principles" />.
- Organizers are not liable for broken computers or other devices.
- Organizers may remove anyone from the event area. If you are removed, your ticket will not be refunded. In special cases, removal may happen immediately.
