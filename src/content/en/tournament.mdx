# Tournaments

The schedule of the 2026 tournaments will be published later.

Semi-formal tournaments are arranged at Haalarilanit. The entrance to the event is free and you may come spectate and express your support on the spot.

## NHL

<Center><Img src="/assets/tournament/NHL.webp" alt="NHL" h={16} /></Center>

This year we're hosting our own 1v1 NHL tournament. Participation is <Mark>free</Mark> for LAN seat holders. Otherwise, the entry fee is <Mark>€5</Mark>, paid on site.

## CS2

<Center><Img src="/assets/tournament/CS2.webp" alt="Counter-Strike 2" h={16} /></Center>
