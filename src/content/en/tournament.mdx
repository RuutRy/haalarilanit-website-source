# Tournaments

The schedules and entry instructions for the 2026 tournaments will be published later.

Semi-formal tournaments are arranged at Haalarilanit, with prizes for the winners. The entrance to the event is free and you may come spectate and express your support on the spot!

## League of Legends

<Center><Img src="/assets/tournament/LoL.webp" alt="League of Legends" w={21.2} /></Center>

The League of Legends tournament will be played on the <Mark>EUW</Mark> server using the Summoner's Rift Tournament Draft 5v5 format. Participation requires <Mark>purchasing a LAN seat and playing on-site at the event</Mark>.

## NHL

<Center><Img src="/assets/tournament/NHL.webp" alt="NHL" w={21.2} /></Center>

This year we're hosting our own 1v1 NHL tournament. Participation is <Mark>free</Mark> for LAN seat holders. Otherwise, the entry fee is <Mark>€5</Mark>, paid on site.

## CS2

<Center><Img src="/assets/tournament/CS2.webp" alt="Counter-Strike 2" h={21.2} /></Center>

The king of LAN games, Counter-Strike, is back once again! The tournament will be played in a 5v5 format on the LAN network using the event servers. Participation requires <Mark>purchasing a LAN seat and playing on-site at the event</Mark>.

## Nintendo Switch

<Center><Img src="/assets/tournament/Switch.webp" alt="Nintendo Switch" h={23.3} /></Center>

Think you're good at multiple Nintendo Switch games? Compete in several of Nintendo's hottest titles and earn points to become the champion. Participation is <Mark>free</Mark> for LAN seat holders. Otherwise, the entry fee is <Mark>€5</Mark>, paid on site.

## WRC

<Center><Img src="/assets/tournament/Wrc.webp" alt="WRC" w={23.2} /></Center>

Flat out through the forest — the World Rally Championship tournament brings rallying to the LAN. Everyone drives the same map in the same car <Mark>in a time trial format</Mark>, with <Mark>two separate attempts</Mark>, and the fastest time wins. Participation is <Mark>free</Mark> for LAN seat holders. Otherwise, the entry fee is <Mark>€5</Mark>, paid on site.
